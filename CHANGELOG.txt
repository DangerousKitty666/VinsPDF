VinsPDF 0.27 — 21 November 2026

Recommended download: VinsPDF-Setup-0.27.exe. Installer distribution is now standard.

- Memory-limited page cache, idle next-page rendering and cached panning margins.
- Compact floating editor with Select, Move selected and Pan modes. Selection
  drags never move objects, even when started over an item.
- Live grid without Snap/Apply, clearer cascading properties, text colour and
  left/centre/right alignment; Add text remains inline and repeated panel show
  is guarded. Removed the redundant Edit text on page command.
- Rotating selection rectangle, centre pointing-hand cursor, dark menus.
- Home / Tools / About / + row; drag tabs out to detach; no separate Open or
  Detach toolbar buttons. Translucent PAGES overview with click-away dismissal.
- Named editor layers with persistent membership, session hide/show and locks.
- Combine PDFs, organise/reorder/remove/rotate pages, extract ranges, export
  original or reduced-size image PDFs, and printing.

Limits: editor layers are page-level text/image groups, not a full PDF optional-
content editor. Hidden groups are included when saving/exporting. Reduced exports
flatten text into images. Page-copy tools may not retain document-level bookmarks,
forms or layer configurations. See README.txt and Performance.txt for validation.
