VinsPDF 0.28 — 21 November 2026

Recommended download: VinsPDF-Setup-0.28.exe. Install over your existing version,
or use About > Check for updates.

- Floating edit panel fits its contents, with a slim dark scrollbar.
- Select mode now supports centre-point movement, eight resize handles and the
  rotation handle. Dragging elsewhere still draws a selection rectangle.
- Resizable on-page text box; compact font/size, bold/italic/underline/strike,
  highlight/colour, list and alignment controls, including justification.
- Removed Rotate selection from the palette; use the circular selection handle.
- Styled Tools dialogs with the VinsPDF logo. Organise pages shows draggable
  thumbnails with rotation and removal controls.
- Export editable or reduced-size PDF, PNG, JPEG, TIFF and BMP. Page ranges,
  resolution, JPEG quality and greyscale settings are included.
- Save changes? on close offers Save, Don't save and Cancel. Pending text is
  saved too; failed or cancelled saves keep the document open.

Text formatting applies to whole items; applied paragraphs become separate lines.
Raster exports flatten content and allow up to 24 megapixels per page. Layers
remain named page-level editor groups; hidden groups are included in exports.
Page-copy tools may omit document-level bookmarks/forms/layer configurations.
See README.txt and Performance.txt for controls, limits and validation.
