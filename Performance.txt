0.29.1 hotfix validation
Reproduced the disposed EditorPalette failure on closing an edited tab in 0.29.
Seven UI lifecycle tests pass after the fix: tab close, window close, cancelled
close, detached tab/old-window close, disposed-panel recovery with queued layer
updates, panel close during inline typing, and save on close. Saved text is
reopened and searched to confirm persistence. Tests run off-screen and log failures
without displaying crash dialogs. The renderer and document engine are unchanged.
